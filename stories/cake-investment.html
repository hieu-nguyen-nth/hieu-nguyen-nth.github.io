Cake by VPBank / NEW VENTURE · INVESTING

Launch stock trading within a broader investment strategy

Turn a product gap into a new investment proposition and revenue line.

The investment launch contributed an estimated $100K in annual revenue. The stock-trading launch was the first step; the broader multi-asset roadmap was phased rather than all launched together.

ContextDecisionExecutionResult

The constraint

Cake’s existing investment proposition was narrow, and a fund partnership was expected to end. Demand for stock investing and other assets created an opportunity, but adding isolated partner products would leave customers with fragmented accounts and portfolio views. The commercial question was how investment could become a stronger reason to keep financial activity inside Cake.

The decision I made

I assessed market trends, competing offers, and behavior from the previous product, then proposed a centralized customer experience backed by specialist partners. Stock trading came first. The initial scope focused on account opening, funding, trading, market information, and watchlists; broader portfolio features and other asset classes were phased later.

What I did

  1. Reframed the replacement decision

    An ending fund partnership exposed a broader gap. I assessed market trends, competitor offers, and prior product behavior instead of sourcing another isolated investment widget.

  2. Defined one experience over specialist partners

    Proposed a centralized customer-facing investment layer, with licensed partners providing the capabilities underneath.

  3. Sequenced a focused first launch

    Prioritized securities account opening, funding, trading, market information, and watchlists. Broader portfolio features and other asset classes stayed in later phases.

  4. Made cross-company delivery concrete

    Mapped identity, account creation, orders, callbacks, reconciliation, and responsibility with the partner; translated the work into wireframes, requirements, milestones, and launch priorities.

What changed

The investment launch contributed an estimated $100K in annual revenue. The stock-trading launch was the first step; the broader multi-asset roadmap was phased rather than all launched together.

New-venture product leadership connects market selection, the commercial model, customer experience, and partner architecture. Sequencing a focused first launch makes a broader platform strategy achievable.
