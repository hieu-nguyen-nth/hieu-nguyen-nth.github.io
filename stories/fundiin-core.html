Fundiin / PLATFORM REDESIGN

Redesign the core while the business keeps running

Own what differentiates the business; integrate what does not.

The redesign enabled 4× loan-volume growth, reduced operating costs by 30%, and halved product time-to-market.

Read alongside my career overview ↗

ContextDecisionExecutionResult

The constraint

Separate app, web, and partner flows duplicated product rules and fragmented data. The inherited core had been built around a different lending behavior and was becoming a constraint on repeated borrowing, new channels, and new products.

The decision I made

I led a hybrid build-versus-buy approach. We built the differentiating lending logic and common product state, integrated standardized identity and payment services, and kept them behind normalized interfaces. We migrated in phases so the business could continue operating.

THE PRODUCT LOGIC

  1. 01Shared product & loan model
  2. 02Reusable lending services
  3. 03Vendor gateways
  4. 04Phased channel migration

What I did

  1. Reconstructed the inherited system

    Worked through app, web, partner, and operational flows with the teams to identify duplicated rules, fragmented state, and constraints on repeated borrowing.

  2. Chose a hybrid build-and-integrate approach

    Kept differentiating lending logic in-house; integrated standardized identity and payment services behind common interfaces instead of building every component.

  3. Turned the target into owned workstreams

    Aligned new engineering leadership, assigned component work to product colleagues, and resolved dependencies across the 25-person cross-functional group.

  4. Made migration an operating decision

    Sequenced the transition while aligning Operations, Finance, and Accounting on data and readiness. The business had to keep running as its foundation changed.

What changed

The redesign enabled 4× loan-volume growth, reduced operating costs by 30%, and halved product time-to-market.

A platform redesign is also an operating-model change. Architecture, ownership, migration, and reconciliation have to work together.
