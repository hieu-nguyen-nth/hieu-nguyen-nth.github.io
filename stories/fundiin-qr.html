Fundiin / 0 → 1 LAUNCH

Launch a new credit behavior through familiar QR payments

Validate a new borrowing behavior before rebuilding for scale.

The product attracted 20K users in its first month and reached 50% of company loan volume within eight months.

Read alongside my career overview ↗

ContextDecisionExecutionResult

The constraint

Fundiin’s merchant-based installment product limited where customers could use credit. QR payments were already familiar to customers and offered a route into more everyday payment situations. The question was whether credit could fit that existing behavior.

The decision I made

I chose an MVP that reused the existing lending system. It accepted constraints in flexibility to test customer demand before committing to a larger overhaul. Discovery combined payment behavior, market and competitor research, and customer research, while eligibility and risk remained part of the launch design.

THE PRODUCT LOGIC

  1. 01Familiar QR payment
  2. 02Eligible credit offer
  3. 03Clear terms & confirmation
  4. 04Usage informs the next investment

What I did

  1. Identified the distribution constraint

    Merchant-by-merchant installment acceptance limited everyday use. I combined QR payment behavior, competitor research, and customer discovery to test a broader credit interaction.

  2. Tested the behavior before rebuilding

    Chose an MVP on the existing lending core, accepting flexibility limits to learn whether customers would understand and use QR-based credit.

  3. Connected experience and risk

    Defined scanning, eligible offers, clear terms, and confirmation while working with leadership on risk appetite and launch decisions.

  4. Gave junior colleagues real ownership

    Coached research, the business case, product artifacts, and developer coordination while remaining accountable for direction and cross-functional delivery.

What changed

The product attracted 20K users in its first month and reached 50% of company loan volume within eight months.

A 0-to-1 launch should test the behavior that matters. Early adoption can then justify investment in the platform needed to support it.
