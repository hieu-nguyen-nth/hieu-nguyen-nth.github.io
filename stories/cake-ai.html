Cake by VPBank / AI PRODUCT

From chat messages to a review-ready transfer

Use AI for interpretation; keep financial authorization explicit.

The constraint

A large part of everyday commerce happens in conversations. Customers receive an account number, amount, or payment description in a message or image, then have to re-enter it in a banking app. The opportunity was to remove that repeated work without turning a language model into the authority to move money.

The decision I made

I separated the experience into understanding the input, preparing a structured transaction, and customer review. The model handled unstructured payment information; banking services and the customer retained their roles in validation and authorization. Voice banking stayed on a separate research and prototype track.

THE PRODUCT LOGIC

  1. 01Message or image
  2. 02AI extracts payment details
  3. 03Structured transfer draft
  4. 04Customer reviews & authorizes

What I did

  1. Started from an everyday behavior

    Customers receive payment details in chats and screenshots. I framed the product around removing re-entry, rather than adding an open-ended banking chatbot.

  2. Drew the AI boundary

    Separated input interpretation and transaction drafting from banking validation and customer authorization. Voice banking stayed on a research/prototype track.

  3. Made prototypes reviewable

    Supplied flows, component rules, typography, and interaction logic to AI-assisted prototyping; brought working drafts to engineers for refinement.

  4. Scaled the method across the team

    Created PRD and ticket workflows using PM-supplied context, including Jira publishing through MCP. Trained 10+ PMs; prototypes that took hours could be prepared in roughly 30 minutes.

What changed

The launched parsing experience correctly prefilled approximately 80% of inputs. I also trained 10+ PMs in AI-assisted prototyping and automation.

AI capability becomes useful when its role in the customer journey is precise. Interpretation, validation, and authorization need different owners.
