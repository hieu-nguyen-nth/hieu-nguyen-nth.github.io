Cake by VPBank / NEW VENTURE · COMMERCE

Build a travel business on an embeddable mini-app platform

Own the customer journey and distribution model, with specialist suppliers behind it.

The in-app travel launch generated $45K in new revenue in its first six months. Its embeddable mini-app architecture enabled independent releases and distribution across banks and partners.

ContextDecisionExecutionResult

The constraint

Cake already offered travel through partner SDKs, but each provider brought its own interface and release dependencies. Cake had limited control over the catalog, add-ons, or customer journey. A banking-app-only feature also limited the opportunity to distribute the same service through other channels.

The decision I made

I reframed the work as a travel-commerce platform Cake could operate and distribute. We would own discovery, booking, payment, and the customer-facing journey, while integrating specialist suppliers through backend adapters. An embeddable mini-app separated routine travel releases from the main native banking app.

THE PRODUCT LOGIC

  1. 01Supplier inventory & adapters
  2. 02Shared booking & payment model
  3. 03Embeddable mini-app
  4. 04Cake and partner distribution

What I did

  1. Challenged the SDK-host model

    Separate supplier interfaces and release schedules limited control of the journey. I combined market opportunity, customer needs, performance, and partner issues into the business case.

  2. Chose what Cake should own

    Kept discovery, booking, payment, and the customer journey under Cake’s control, while integrating specialist inventory through backend adapters.

  3. Designed for more than one host

    Used an embeddable mini-app and common booking model so travel releases could move independently of the native banking app and support other distribution channels.

  4. Kept the launch focused

    Led partner solution design, wireframes, requirements, and readiness around a flights MVP. Sequenced additional services and post-booking capabilities later.

What changed

The in-app travel launch generated $45K in new revenue in its first six months. Its embeddable mini-app architecture enabled independent releases and distribution across banks and partners.

The product decision changed both delivery and economics: independent releases, a consistent customer journey, and a distribution capability that could extend beyond one host app.
