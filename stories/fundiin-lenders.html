Fundiin / PLATFORM EXPANSION

Build a scalable multi-lender platform

Build the lending infrastructure for a growing network of financial partners.

The launch expanded monthly funding capacity from $2M to $20M across three financial institutions. The platform now connects 5+ lenders. Funding capacity measures available capital, not actual monthly disbursement.

Read alongside my career overview ↗

ContextDecisionExecutionResult

The constraint

Growth depended on access to lending capital. Limited funding and partner concentration meant that stronger customer demand alone could not expand the business. Each new institution also brought different product models, fund flows, APIs, and accounting needs.

The decision I made

I designed lender integration as a scalable platform capability. A common internal loan model and institution-specific adapters separated shared product behavior from partner differences. We launched with one lender while establishing the architecture for additional institutions.

THE PRODUCT LOGIC

  1. 01Common customer & loan model
  2. 02Lender matching & orchestration
  3. 03Institution-specific adapters
  4. 045+ connected lenders

What I did

  1. Located the real growth ceiling

    Customer demand could not grow the business without capital. I studied lender structures and translated funding concentration into a platform business case.

  2. Defined common models and partner boundaries

    Led product design around a shared internal loan model, matching and routing, and institution-specific adapters instead of rebuilding core flows for each lender.

  3. Worked through the money movement

    Aligned banks, payment providers, Finance, Legal, Risk, and Engineering on disbursement, repayment, fees, accounting entries, and reconciliation.

  4. Protected delivery when knowledge was leaving

    Mapped critical components threatened by engineering attrition and escalated a continuity plan to the CEO and CTO. Retained enough domain knowledge to finish delivery and transfer context.

What changed

The launch expanded monthly funding capacity from $2M to $20M across three financial institutions. The platform now connects 5+ lenders. Funding capacity measures available capital, not actual monthly disbursement.

A scalable platform expands the business’s options: more capital partners, less dependence on one institution, and a common foundation for new lending products.
