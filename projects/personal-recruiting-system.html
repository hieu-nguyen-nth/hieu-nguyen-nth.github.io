Personal Recruiting System

A personal recruiting workflow designed to preserve context: learn once, reuse evidence, and resume work without reconstructing it from scattered chats.

CodexReusable skillsNotionMCP
ProblemSystem flowDecisionsOutcome

The product problem

Job discovery, company research, relationship notes, and resume preparation often become separate activities. Two roles at one company can lead to duplicate research; a new chat can lose the last decision; a polished draft can drift beyond the candidate’s actual experience. The product problem was continuity and evidence, not the absence of another application.

The approach

I designed a coordinator that routes work through reusable methods for evaluation, company research, networking, tailoring, and QA. Notion holds durable Jobs, Companies, People, and Events records. Candidate evidence stays in private source files. The public repository is a reference implementation with a fully fictional worked case, allowing readers to inspect the method without exposing personal recruiting records.

SYSTEM FLOW

One request, shared context, explicit handoffs

The coordinator runs through an assistant. Methods are instructions, not a custom rules engine; connectors and shared records provide the operational tools and state.

PRODUCT & SYSTEM DECISIONS

Use a durable information model

One company can link to many jobs. Company research is reused; individual conversations stay with the person, and shared event insights remain on the event. That reduces duplicated context and preserves the reason behind a follow-up.

Separate research from candidate evaluation

A company can be understood without a specific job or resume. Role fit and application positioning are separate tasks, so the same business research remains useful across opportunities.

Keep generation and review distinct

Tailoring proposes changes from evidence. QA reviews the actual draft against the job and original stories. A separate pass can catch shared assumptions, but it is not a claim of a separate evaluator model or guaranteed truth.

Prefer methods over unnecessary infrastructure

The system uses an assistant, connectors, private files, and Notion. No custom website or continuously running coordinator is required. Bounded scans record coverage and unfinished work; configured automation still needs actual execution checks.

What the work shows

The fictional case follows two roles at one company through evaluation, research, relationship context, supported edits, and QA corrections. It makes the handoffs and rejected claims inspectable. The repository demonstrates the workflow design; it does not contain a live installation, account configuration, or the user’s private candidate records.

The value of an agent workflow is the quality of its handoffs: what it reads, what it changes, what it verifies, and what the human decides.
