Afield

A research companion built around a personal problem: too many places to keep up, and a 45-minute trip across the Bay that could become time to learn.

The personal starting point

My reading was scattered across newsletters, email, blogs, and news sites. Saving more links did not make it easier to connect the ideas or keep up with a topic. A roughly 45-minute trip across the Bay created another opportunity: I could listen when reading a screen was impractical. I wanted the experience to fit my interests and available time, rather than another feed to scroll.

The approach

I designed two connected journeys. Explore answers a specific question; Stay Updated follows a topic over time. AI suggests subtopics and recurring learning sources, while the reader chooses what to follow. The reader can set depth, source coverage, reusable briefing blocks, and a reading or listening budget. The output is a saved, cited text brief with audio available on demand. The current source layer reads public pages and feeds.

PRODUCT & SYSTEM DECISIONS

Personalization is a conversation

Suggested interests and sources are starting points. The reader can accept, reject, or add their own; selected interests survive another suggestion round.

Research quality starts before writing

Source suggestions examine publisher evidence and recurring learning value. Separate page, feed, linked-page, and site-search modes make the intended coverage explicit.

Audio is a deliberate product choice

Text comes first. A listener requests speech when needed; matching verified audio can be reused. A listening-time target is an estimate until the generated recording is measured.

A web request should not own a long research job

The Next.js app accepts the request and saves its state. A separate worker handles retrieval, generation, audio, and schedules, so progress and failures can be revisited.

Where it stands

The implementation connects topic and source suggestions, cited briefs, saved history, recurring settings, and requested audio. The repository includes product decisions and walkthroughs using synthetic data. Live deployment is on the way.

The useful unit is a learning experience that fits a person’s interests, attention, and time—not simply a shorter article.
